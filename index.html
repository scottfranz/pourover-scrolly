The funk cliff

Funky is fine, until it isn't

Ratings hold steady through moderate funk, then fall off at clean/funk 8+: 7.66 for bags at 6 or below (n=108), 4.58 for 8 and up (n=6).

The high-funk group is small, so treat its shape as a sketch.
